# Dreamfold — Interactive World Model

Team: **openspace lab**

**Coming Soon**

One scene. Two actions. Two futures.

Give the world an action. Let the future unfold.

Dreamfold explores an interactive, egocentric world model: describe a scene in natural language, provide VR hand actions, and generate what happens next.

This preview presents two prerecorded tabletop puzzle videos under different action conditions. We are exploring how a generated world can respond to the user's actions.

Video previews are available now. The full project overview, technical details, and interactive demo are coming soon. A release date has not been announced.
